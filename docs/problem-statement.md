# 🎯 Problem Statement

## The Real-World Cyber Fraud Investigation Challenge
Cybercrime and UPI fraud investigations often involve fragmented, disconnected intelligence:
- Bank transaction statements (.csv, .xlsx)
- Phone numbers and SIM card IMSI logs
- Device IMEI identifiers and hardware IDs
- IP addresses and login logs
- Witness complaints and field officer notes (.pdf, .txt)

## The Core Pain Point
Currently, investigators manually trace relationships across hundreds of separate Excel spreadsheets and text reports. Discovering that **Person A uses Phone X, which accesses Device Z, which transferred funds through Mule Account B to Crypto Account C** is slow, error-prone, and allows fraud networks to move funds before detection.

## Who Experiences This Problem?
- Cyber Crime Cells & Digital Forensics Analysts
- Bank Fraud Risk Management Teams
- Financial Intelligence & Anti-Money Laundering Officers
